## Description (Markdown mode; replace PASTE_IMAGE_LINK with the hero screenshot URL)

```markdown
Pedibus is for groups that travel together. One player drives, the others click **Board** and auto-follow them.

![Driver panel and a poker table](PASTE_IMAGE_LINK)

## The bus

- Click the floating button, pick the driver, click **Board**. You now auto-follow them.
- The driver gets a panel with each passenger's distance. Anyone past 40 yards gets a sound and an on-screen warning, on both sides.
- Auto-follow breaks as soon as you move. The **Follow** button on your badge, or a key binding, turns it back on.
- Your badge has an arrow pointing to the driver. It hides in instances, where the game hides positions.
- **Stop**: the driver announces a stop, each passenger clicks **I'm done**, and the driver hears a sound when everyone is ready. Passengers can ask for a stop with **Stop?**.
- **Roll call** re-invites people who haven't boarded and gives passengers a Follow button.

## Games for passengers

Auto-follow leaves you with only the mouse, so the games are mouse-only. Right-click the Pedibus button to open them.

- Yahtzee, 1 to 4 players
- Battleship, 2 players
- Connect 4, 2 players
- Texas Hold'em, 2 to 5 players, with chips (no gold)

Other group members can watch a game in progress; in poker, hole cards stay hidden. When someone opens a game, you get a bubble with a Join button. Games pause at stops.

## Languages

English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, following your game client. Players using different languages can share a bus and a game. Some translations may be off: corrections are welcome in the comments.

## Requirements and limits

- Everyone in the group needs Pedibus. The panel shows who has it, and **Send link** posts the download link in group chat.
- You need to be in a party or raid.
- The game blocks addon messages during boss fights, Mythic+ and PvP. Pedibus tells you and waits.
- Commands: `/pedibus`, `/pedibus games`, `/pedibus debug`, `/pedibus arrow`.

Bugs and translation errors: leave a comment.

```
